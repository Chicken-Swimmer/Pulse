Pulse watches URLs you care about — home-server dashboards, Immich, Jellyfin, a blog — and notifies you when they stop answering.

It is a GPL-3.0 fork of Website Monitor. Your list never leaves the phone.

Features
• Scheduled checks (15 minutes or longer in the background)
• Retries before a down alert
• No false “down” when the phone is offline
• Airplane mode with Wi-Fi still checks
• Quiet hours and pause-until-morning
• Optional notification when a site recovers
• History, uptime and latency on each site
• Home-screen widgets, including a muted variant
• JSON backup and restore

Permissions
• Internet — to reach the URLs you added
• Notifications — down / back-up alerts
• Exact alarms (optional) — more reliable checks on some phones

No account. No analytics.
