# Privacy Policy — Pulse

Last update: 14 September 2026

Pulse is a free, open-source uptime monitor. It is a GPL-3.0 fork of Website Monitor by Manimaran.

## What Pulse stores

All sites, check history and settings stay on this phone. Pulse does not create an account and does not send your list to a Pulse server.

When a check runs, the phone contacts only the URLs you added (and, for icons, public favicon helpers if that feature is used). Those sites see a normal request from your network, the same as opening the page in a browser.

## What Pulse does not do

- No analytics, ads or crash-reporting SDKs
- No sale or rental of data
- No login

## Links

A site you monitor is a third-party site. Their own privacy policy applies when Pulse requests that URL.
